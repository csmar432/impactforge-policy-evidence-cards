# Devpost handoff

1. Sign in to Devpost and confirm the account display name.
2. Confirm legal-age, student, residence, and non-company eligibility under the current Rules.
3. Register for ImpactForge from the challenge page, then create the project submission.
4. Copy the prepared fields from `DEVPOST.md` and `IMPACT_STATEMENT.md`.
5. Add the public repository and live demo links.
6. Upload `impactforge-mvp-runtime-desktop.png` and `impactforge-mvp-interaction-desktop.png`; add `impactforge-demo.webm` if the form accepts the file directly.
7. Confirm the team entry as solo and describe the contribution from `DEVPOST.md`.
8. Resolve the deadline conflict and complete the real-student test before the final submit action.

Do not claim synthetic usability results as real-user evidence. Do not click the final submit action until the deadline and eligibility are confirmed.
