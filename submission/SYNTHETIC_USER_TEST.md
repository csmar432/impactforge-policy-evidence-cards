# Synthetic usability preflight

Date: 2026-08-16

This is a read-only, five-persona simulation performed by a subagent. It is not a study with real students and must not be reported as real-user evidence.

## Personas and outcomes

- Hurried first-year student on 320px mobile: found the topic, 59% scope, USDA rules, next-step chooser, and reader check, but required a long scroll.
- Student journalist on 320px mobile: found GAO and USDA sources and correctly scoped 59%; the original visual hierarchy made the denominator easy to miss during a fast scan.
- Campus adviser on desktop: found the official USDA rules; the page now also states that personal guidance belongs with a campus adviser or state benefits office.
- Skeptical student checking 59%: could verify the limitation; the denominator is now placed directly beside the percentage.
- Low-confidence reader: understood the sequential choices; “Not yet” now reveals direct definition and source links for recovery.

## Result

The simulation led to a denominator clarification, mobile quick links, an actionable “Not yet” response, clearer federal-rules wording, and explicit brand focus styling. These changes improve the prototype but do not complete the required real-student test.
