# Impact Statement

Policy Evidence Cards helps students read public policy evidence without needing to decode a 30-page report first. The first brief focuses on student food insecurity and makes three things visible in sequence: the scale of the issue, the severity of the reported experience, and the gap between potential SNAP eligibility and reported receipt.

The direct beneficiaries are students, campus advisers, educators, and student journalists who need a trustworthy starting point. The prototype is intentionally small: it does not decide eligibility or claim to replace a campus support office. It gives people a source-linked explanation, names the data year, and shows the limits of the claim before pointing to the official USDA student rules.

The longer-term impact is a reusable format for turning complex public data into better questions and more informed next steps.
