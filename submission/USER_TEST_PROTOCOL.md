# Five-minute reader test

This protocol is ready for a real student tester. Do not fill in results without an actual person using the page.

## Setup

Open the public project link on a phone. Do not explain the page before the test. Ask the tester to think aloud, but do not correct them until the end.

## Tasks

1. “Read this page and tell me what problem it is about.”
2. “What does the 59% number apply to?”
3. “If you wanted to check what help might be available, where would you click?”

## Success threshold

The MVP passes when at least 4 of 5 testers can correctly scope the 59% statistic and find the official next-step link without coaching.

## Privacy

Do not collect names, contact details, food-security status, benefit status, or other sensitive information. This is a comprehension test, not a needs assessment.
